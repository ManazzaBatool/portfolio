Where Enterprise Security & SaaS Deals Stall

Three commercial bottlenecks diagnosed and resolved across high-ACV enterprise pipelines.

BOTTLENECK 01 // INCUMBENT BUNDLING

The "We Already Get That Bundled" Default

The CISO likes your security product, then procurement points to an existing Microsoft E5, CrowdStrike, or CyberArk agreement covering 70% of the checklist at zero marginal cost. Pushing a 30-row feature matrix validates a comparison you cannot win on depth.

BOTTLENECK 02 // SECURITY REVIEW DRAG

The Passive Trust Center & Spreadsheet Trap

Holding SOC 2 or ISO 27001 does not close enterprise SaaS deals when static compliance logos still trigger 300-row custom questionnaires, or when your CTO improvises answers around subprocessor boundaries and EU DORA resilience controls.

BOTTLENECK 03 // COMMITTEE VETO

The Dual-Buyer Narrative Collapse

The CISO evaluates balance-sheet risk and regulatory liability, while Platform Engineering vetoes over CPU overhead, latency, or deployment friction. Without one narrative that reconciles both stakeholders, internal champions go silent.
