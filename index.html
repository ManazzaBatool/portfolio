Where Enterprise Security Deals Stall

Three commercial friction points diagnosed and resolved across enterprise sales pipelines.

FRICTION POINT 01

The Bundled Contract Default

The CISO likes the product, then procurement points to an existing Microsoft or CrowdStrike agreement covering 70% of the checklist at zero marginal cost. Pushing feature matrices validates the comparison you cannot win on depth.

FRICTION POINT 02

The Claim That Invites a Rebuttal

Reps claim exclusivity on broad capabilities. The incumbent forwards a public data sheet showing they also cover it, freezing the deal. Displacement cards identify which claims hold up and which hand the competitor an easy counter.

FRICTION POINT 03

The Dual-Buyer Collapse

The CISO buys risk containment, but Platform Engineering vetoes over deployment friction or pipeline latency. Without a narrative that reconciles both stakeholders in one message, internal champions go silent.
