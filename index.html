Where Enterprise Deals Slip Through Messaging

Three friction points I look for in cybersecurity sales and marketing copy.

FRICTION POINT 01

The "Engineering Dump" Trap

Brilliant low-level telemetry described as "multi-tenant eBPF distributed engine" loses CISOs fast. They don't buy code; they buy lower risk and fewer midnight incidents.

FRICTION POINT 02

The Unwinnable Demo Call

When copy fails to pre-educate buyers, sales reps spend the first 20 minutes explaining what the product does instead of closing.

FRICTION POINT 03

The Commodity Vacuum

"AI-powered, unified, proactive security" reads the same as 200 other vendors. Without sharp differentiation, buyers default to familiar incumbents.
