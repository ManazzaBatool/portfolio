Where Enterprise Deals Slip Through Messaging

Three friction points I diagnose and resolve in cybersecurity sales and marketing copy.

FRICTION POINT 01

The "Engineering Dump" Trap

Brilliant low-level telemetry described as "multi-tenant eBPF distributed engine" loses CISOs fast. They don't buy code; they buy lower risk, board-level compliance, and fewer midnight incident calls.

FRICTION POINT 02

The Unwinnable Demo Call

When copy fails to pre-educate buyers on architecture, AEs spend the first 20 minutes explaining baseline vocabulary instead of driving discovery and setting proof-of-concept timelines.

FRICTION POINT 03

The Commodity Vacuum

"AI-powered, unified, proactive security" sounds identical to 200 other vendors. Without sharp operational contrast, risk-averse buyers default to familiar legacy incumbents.
